Caveats

  • One checkpoint and chords between correctly classified images. The minimum raw sharpness index across the six starts was {{fact:x.x4-paths.sharp.min}}. At finer resolution, the largest site-wise median absolute change in angular window share was {{fact:x.x4-paths.refine.max_median}} (1,024 vs 256 steps on 50 paths per start); that is not a bound for individual paths.
  • audit The late class-mass slope depends on the distribution of probe images and on the adaptive row weights; final row perplexity decreases late in the suffix. The experiment does not separate these effects or check a matched-width kernel.
  • audit Different-class paths can leave the supported parts of the probe cloud, especially at late sites. The report's worst-support comparison takes the minimum over each path, not just its midpoint.
  • audit Even on the b9-start affine control, the angular speed peak can align with a class switch. Centred logit radius can be small there, raising angular speed without raising raw speed.
  • Largest two-image squared-norm shares among the retained modes, joint / site-local: pixels {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.pixels.joint}} / {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.pixels.site}}, b3 {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.b3.joint}} / {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.b3.site}}, b6 {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.b6.joint}} / {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.b6.site}}, b9 {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.b9.joint}} / {{fact:x.x4-paths.loc.b9.site}}. Localised modes may affect the projected paths and embedded path lengths in the full report. The activation, gain and prediction results here use no modes. The class-mass slopes use kernel rows, not modes, but are kernel-dependent.
