How much of an activation can K coordinates recover?

Tested: one CIFAR-10 ResNet-20 checkpoint, 2,000 held-out test images (H). The headline results use the 8,000-image probe P8 with participation-ratio target 400 (pr400). The embedding below uses the 2,000-image probe P2 with pr300; both operators have 12 sites.

Kernel ridge recovers direction much better at pool than at an intermediate block. With 64 modes on P8 pr400, mean centred cosine is {{fact:x.inv-decode.large.b4}} at b4 and {{fact:x.inv-decode.large.pool}} at pool. Kernel ridge led the tested closed-form decoders by held-out cosine, but the trained MLP gave higher patched top-1 agreement at b4 ({{fact:x.inv-decode.large.b4_mlp}} vs {{fact:x.inv-decode.large.b4_agree}}).

What was computed

The input $z$ is the first $K$ joint coordinates at one site; $Z$ holds the probe inputs. Except where noted, $X_c$ holds flattened probe activations centred on the probe mean $\mu$, without unit normalisation. A weight row $W(z)$ predicts $\hat x=\mu+W(z)X_c$.

Yes, the gh decoder is the latent-harmonics step of Evangelou et al. (2022 preprint; 2023 journal article): a second Gaussian kernel on the latent coordinates, a truncated eigen-expansion and Nyström extension. The first stage here takes the lowest modes in the slice-mean-zero subspace of a depth-coupled operator built from balanced activation kernels, with out-of-sample extension via the depth solve. It does not use the paper’s single-data-cloud diffusion map with nonharmonic coordinates selected for a manifold parametrisation. Here the second kernel retains modes by relative eigenvalue, and kernel ridge, not latent harmonics, had the higher mean held-out cosine.

Caveats

  • Audit correction: The decoder’s inner validation targets are probe images whose coordinates came from an operator fitted on the full probe. H was disjoint from every fit but informed the recommendations; no fresh test or repeat over seeds was run.
  • Audit correction: On P2 pr300 (not the P8 pr400 headline run), {{fact:x.inv-decode.near.count}} H images have row max > 0.5 at some site; {{fact:x.inv-decode.near.top20}} of the 20 largest b4 joint norms at K = 64 are among them. An unmasked near-copy can overshoot when the probe operator has a zero diagonal. The row-max flag is a diagnostic, not a verified duplicate label.
  • Probe size and kernel width change together. The neighbouring P2 and P8 curves do not isolate the effect of adding images.
  • Neither centred cosine nor the PCA reference is a ceiling on how well the network’s suffix will behave. Decodes away from the probe are extrapolations.
