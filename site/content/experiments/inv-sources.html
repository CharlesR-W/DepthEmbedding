Is a joint coordinate at a site a function of that site's activation?

Tested: Final CIFAR-10 ResNet-20, seed 0. The attack uses the P2 participation-ratio 300 operator. The filter-weight and patch comparisons also show results for the larger P8 participation-ratio 400 operator.

The joint-coordinate formula uses sources from the whole chain; the source-coordinate formula uses only that site's activation. Source coordinate$s_{l,k}(x)=\sum_i p_l(x_l,i)f^{(k)}_l(i)$
On the probe: $s_{l,k}=P_lf_l^{(k)}$.		$f^{(k)}$ is one joint mode fit on the probe, $p_l$ the new image's balanced row from its activation $x_l$. Source coordinates use only that row at inference; their basis was fit jointly.modal_invert.py:joint, stage_rt.py:run (FS)
Depth filter$T_k=\operatorname{diag}((2c_l)^{-1}+t\deg_l-\mu_k)-tA_{\rm path}$
$y_{\cdot,k}=T_k^{-1}(s_{\cdot,k}/(2c))$		$\mu_k$ is the joint eigenvalue, $c_l$ the local scale, $t$ the coupling. $A_{\rm path}$ links consecutive sites, with degrees $(1,2,\ldots,2,1)$. Divide each source by its own $2c_l$ before solving. This filter is fixed by the operator.dm.py:joint_forward
Own-source share$w_{lm,k}=|[T_k^{-1}]_{lm}|/(2c_m)$
$\mathrm{share}_{l,k}=w_{ll,k}/\sum_m w_{lm,k}$		At site $l$, take the diagonal coefficient's share of the absolute row weight. Average ratios over the first $K$ modes. This is not a share of observed variance or a regression coefficient.stage_rt.py:run (E1|share_ll)
Source-on-joint slope$\hat\beta_{l,k}=\sum_i s_{l,k}(i)y_{l,k}(i)/\sum_i y_{l,k}(i)^2$Through-origin fit from joint to source, separately per mode over the first $m$ held-out H images, then averaged across modes. No patch or new forward pass.stage_rt.py:run (E1|slope)
Patched-run progress$d=(y_b-y_a)/\sigma$, $\mathrm{prog}_D=\langle(y'-y_a)/\sigma,d\rangle/\|d\|^2$At the patched site, project the displacement toward target image b, relative to clean held-out per-mode spread $\sigma$. To compute $y'$, use image a's prefix, the edited activation at the patch, and the recomputed suffix, then apply the filter. Average over pairs with different original network predictions.stage_rt.py:run (FD, progress)
Attack displacement$\operatorname{mean}_{i,k}\big[((u^{\rm adv}_{l,k}(i)-u^{\rm clean}_{l,k}(i))/\sigma_{l,k})^2\big]$$u$ is joint or source. Standardise by the clean pairs' spread, separately at each site and mode and for each kind. Two independent draws from the same distribution have expected squared displacement 2 in these units.adv.py; cutoff check in gen_inv_a.py

Caveats

  • Audit: The source-on-joint slope uses held-out H, not probe images, and is distinct from progress under a patch. The pilot's swaps with other sources held fixed are also distinct from the plotted patch runs, which recompute the suffix.
  • Audit: The source-coordinate class readout in the full attack report uses centroids from the second half of H; the joint readout uses probe centroids. No class readout enters the displacement calculation here.
  • The late-source cutoff is a calculation in a fixed linear filter. It is not a causal intervention on the network. Joint and source distances use their own normalisations.
  • One network, checkpoint and probe draw per size. No uncertainty intervals, and the patch methods are not matched in size.
