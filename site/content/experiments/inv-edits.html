Can an activation be given another image's source coordinates, and does the output follow?

Tested: one CIFAR-10 ResNet-20 checkpoint, P8 pr400, K = 16 and 64. Each intervention changes one activation and recomputes the suffix.

At b4, matching the donor’s source nearly exactly made the patched joint match slightly worse. The donor’s class probability barely moved. Even this source match used the donor’s actual activation as an oracle.

Caveats

  • The cohort is the first {{fact:x.inv-edits.sample.cohort}} rows of sorted H (through test index {{fact:x.inv-edits.sample.last_index}}). Donors start one position back cyclically; later shifts apply only when clean predictions agree. There are {{fact:x.inv-edits.sample.pairs}} valid pairs and {{fact:x.inv-edits.sample.donors}} distinct donors, with maximum reuse {{fact:x.inv-edits.sample.max_reuse}}. Neither the pairs nor the sites are independent replicates.
  • Against the corresponding P2 archive slice, b4 probabilities and sizes replay exactly. The maximum deterministic b6 discrepancies were below {{fact:x.inv-edits.replay.bound_p_b}} in probability and {{fact:x.inv-edits.replay.bound_size}} in relative size. b6 random directions were redrawn.

Method concerns from the audit

  • audit Limit — sources lose radial scale and late-site rank. A positive rescaling about the probe centre preserves the source. The local Jacobian rank is at most {{fact:x.inv-edits.rank.pool}} at pool and {{fact:x.inv-edits.rank.logits}} at logits, short of K = 64. Measure singular values; a match cannot certify recovery of the donor activation.
  • audit Limit — a solver miss is not infeasibility. An actual donor activation is a feasible witness without bounds. A midpoint or extrapolation need not have any realising activation. From a, the K = 64 Euclidean donor misses were {{fact:x.inv-edits.rank.pool_miss}} at pool and {{fact:x.inv-edits.rank.logits_miss}} at logits. Report cap/radius hits and donor distance, and separate oracle-feasible from synthetic requests.
  • audit Possible flaw — edits need not look like real activations. Even probe-span displacements can give off-support endpoints or violate post-activation sign constraints. Report norm changes, sign violations and distance to real held-out activations.
  • audit Limit — the random control does not isolate matching. At P8 b4 K = 64, mean relative sizes were {{fact:x.inv-edits.b4.size_before}} for the residual guess and {{fact:x.inv-edits.b4.size_after}} with matching. The headline random draw starts at a and matches the whole guess, not the extra matching step. Compare paired random increments from the guess, covariance-aware noise and probability-versus-budget curves.
