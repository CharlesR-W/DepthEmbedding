Can an activation be given another image's source coordinates, and does the output follow?

Tested: one CIFAR-10 ResNet-20 checkpoint, P8 pr400 (8,000 probe images), at K = 16 and 64. Pairs from the first 256 held-out images were kept only when the two clean predictions differ. Each intervention changes just one activation and then recomputes the suffix.

Matching another image’s source coordinates need not transfer its prediction. At b4, K = 64, matching from the residual guess reduces the mean squared source miss from {{fact:x.inv-edits.b4.miss_before}} to {{fact:x.inv-edits.b4.miss_after}}, but b’s class receives only {{fact:x.inv-edits.b4.match}} mean probability, versus {{fact:x.inv-edits.b4.residual}} from the guess alone. The numbers below are mean probability mass, not the fraction of pairs that switch to b’s class.

On this rerun slice, adding matching at b4 cut the full-dimensional mean source miss from {{fact:x.inv-edits.replay.b4.residual_miss}} to {{fact:x.inv-edits.replay.b4.match_miss}}. The mean probability of b’s class was {{fact:x.inv-edits.replay.b4.residual}} for the guess and {{fact:x.inv-edits.replay.b4.match}} after matching.

A source uses only that site’s activation and the corresponding joint-mode slice $f_l$. Matching step$u_*=J^\top(JJ^\top+\lambda I)^{-1}(s_*-s_l(x)+Ju)$
$\lambda=10^{-6}\operatorname{mean}\operatorname{diag}(JJ^\top)$		$u$ is the current displacement in the search coordinates from the start $x_0$; $J$ differentiates the source in those coordinates. Recompute it by autograd including the implicit width derivative $\partial_d\beta=-\partial_d H_2/\partial_\beta H_2$ for $H_2(\beta,d)=\log(\mathrm{PR})$. The damped linearised candidate is measured from $x_0$, not a free increment.stage_rt:crit_and_slope, bisect_beta, Site.rows, match
Search metric and bounds$x=x_0+u$ or $x=x_0+G^\top u$Euclidean matching searches directly in activation space; probe-metric matching penalises coefficient norm with $G=X_c/\sqrt N$. Both use an activation-space step cap $0.5\sigma_0$ and radius $3\sigma_0$ from their own start; $\sigma_0$ is the mean centred activation norm on the held-out batch at that site. Twenty iterations; at most six trials per iteration, halving failed ones and accepting only a non-increasing unscaled source residual.stage_rt:match
Miss and requests$K^{-1}\sum_k((s_{l,k}(x')-s_{*,k})/\sigma_k)^2$
$s_*=(1-\tau)s_l(x_a)+\tau s_l(x_b)$		$\sigma_k$ is the sample standard deviation of mode $k$ over the held-out batch, measured at that site. E4 targets b ($\tau=1$); E5 requests the midpoint ($\tau=0.5$) or an extrapolation ($\tau=1.5$).stage_rt:srel, run (E4, E5)
Edit size and random control$\|x'-x_a\|_2/\|x_b-x_a\|_2$Size is relative to a full activation swap. The random control uses an isotropic unit direction scaled to the corresponding method’s actual move for each pair.stage_rt:run (E4)

Caveats

  • Audit correction: Damped matching reduces the residual subject to a step cap and trust radius; it does not certify the nearest pre-image, and an extrapolation miss does not prove infeasibility.
  • Making the local source agree does not force the joint coordinate of the patched run to agree. The joint coordinate also depends on the unchanged prefix and the recomputed suffix.
  • Methods take different-sized steps; the comparison is at their natural edit sizes. The random control is isotropic, not covariance-matched.
  • The true source of b and the full swap are oracle comparisons. They do not solve the problem of editing to a source inferred from an arbitrary joint coordinate.
  • Pairs were selected by different clean predictions, not by ground-truth labels or a random draw over all image pairs. This is one checkpoint and probe draw.
  • The embedding figures are a 64-pair P2 pr300 rerun at K = 16 on a different GPU type. At b4, probabilities and sizes replay exactly; b6 deterministic methods agree to within $6\times10^{-4}$, but b6 random edits are new norm-matched draws, not the archived directions. The main tiles and charts still use the full P8 pr400 archive.
