Several natural-image pipelines gave similar embeddings on the same CIFAR-10 probe; SVHN was further away, already at the stem. Some late agreement survives shuffling image identities within class, even as exact neighbour overlap falls.

Caveats

  • Audit correction: This compares pipelines, not the effect of changing data alone. Class counts, images, test accuracy, augmentation, training exposure and normalisation can change together. The coarse-label control tests one change of labels on fixed images, not all changes.
  • Audit correction: Rankings can change with depth and probe. On SVHN at b9, CIFAR-10 agrees more with the animal and vehicle subsets than with ImageNet-32. See the cross-probe table below.
  • One architecture, usually two runs per pipeline. No probe bootstrap or equivalence test was reported; a seed pair is a reference, not a ceiling.
  • The rotations replay the saved reference-panel cosines to {{fact:x.x1-data.replay.raw}} maximum absolute error on the raw modes. On the shipped int8 coordinates, the same rotations give a maximum error of {{fact:x.x1-data.replay.i8}} before display projection. Headers show the original scores.
