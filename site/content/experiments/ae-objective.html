What the autoencoders group by

A classifier's neighbourhoods fill up with the image's own class, from {{fact:x.ae-objective.readout.clf.stem}} at the stem to {{fact:x.ae-objective.readout.clf.b9}} at b9. The autoencoders' stay between {{fact:x.ae-objective.readout.ae.min}} and {{fact:x.ae-objective.readout.ae.max}} at every site: above random ({{fact:x.ae-objective.readout.chance}}), at about the class purity of images that look alike at 4 × 4 pixels. This is a property of the neighbourhoods, not a classification accuracy: it does not show that the label cannot be read from the autoencoders' activations, only that an image's nearest images are not mostly of its class.

The autoencoders' neighbours lean towards coarse appearance. In the 10-number autoencoders {{fact:x.ae-objective.thumb.ae10.stem}} of an image's neighbours are also among its nearest thumbnails at the stem, {{fact:x.ae-objective.thumb.ae10.b3}} at b3 and {{fact:x.ae-objective.thumb.ae10.b9}} at b9; in the classifiers the share starts at {{fact:x.ae-objective.thumb.clf.stem}} and falls to {{fact:x.ae-objective.thumb.clf.b9}}. This is a preference, not an account of every neighbour: most of an autoencoder's b9 neighbours are not nearest thumbnails.

What the autoencoders keep

These are weak autoencoders. Test PSNR (peak signal-to-noise ratio of the reconstruction; higher is better) is {{fact:x.ae-objective.psnr.ae10_s0}} dB with a 10-number code and {{fact:x.ae-objective.psnr.ae64_s0}} dB with 64 numbers. That is barely above principal component analysis of the same rank ({{fact:x.ae-objective.psnr.pca10}} and {{fact:x.ae-objective.psnr.pca64}} dB) and well above outputting the mean training image ({{fact:x.ae-objective.psnr.mean_image}} dB). PCA is a reference, not a target: it is a linear map of the pixels themselves, positions included, while the code passes through a pool that averages positions away. In these examples the 10-number code keeps the colour layout (sky, ground, a dark or bright blob where the object is) and little shape; 64 numbers add rough outlines.

The two networks

Within each seed, both kinds were built by the same initialisation code and trained on the same batch order with the same crops and flips, under the classifier's optimiser settings (SGD, learning rate {{fact:x.ae-objective.train.lr}}, {{fact:x.ae-objective.train.steps}} steps). Before training, a fresh same-seed classifier build matched the 10-number autoencoder's network tensor by tensor ({{fact:x.ae-objective.preflight.init.tensors_10}} tensors, last layer included) and the 64-number one's through the trunk ({{fact:x.ae-objective.preflight.init.tensors_64}} tensors; its last layer is new); both also matched the zoo's step-0 checkpoints. The retrained classifiers' own starting states were not saved. Pairs of different seeds, which carry the headline, share neither starting weights nor batch order.

What differs is what follows the last layer. The autoencoder's decoder ({{fact:x.ae-objective.params.dec10}} parameters, against {{fact:x.ae-objective.params.enc10}} in the ResNet) must redraw the crop it was given from the code alone. The code is computed from b9 averaged over image positions, so where things are survives only as far as the channels themselves encode it; the decoder has to rebuild the layout from 10 or 64 numbers.

The last layer is shown apart in the figures. The decoder starts with a linear layer, which can absorb any invertible linear change of the code, so the code's geometry is not fixed by the objective and comparing it with a classifier's logits says little.

Caveats

  • More changed than the loss. The comparison is class prediction against reconstruction of the augmented crop through a learned decoder behind a pooled bottleneck. The 64-number code is a check on the code's width, not a test of whether the loss or the bottleneck causes the difference.
  • Weak autoencoders. They barely beat PCA. A stronger one, for example with a decoder that reads b9 before pooling, might organise its trunk differently.
  • Two seeds of each kind. Bands are ranges over two or four pairs, or over two networks; permutation references are single draws; nothing here is a significance test.
  • One probe, one architecture, one checkpoint. {{fact:x.ae-objective.sample.probe}} CIFAR-10 test images, ResNet-20, final weights only.
  • One notion of neighbour. 15 nearest by centred cosine, which discards the length of each activation; other neighbourhood sizes and distances were not tried.
  • The label readout is not a probe. Low purity does not mean the class cannot be decoded from the autoencoders' activations.
