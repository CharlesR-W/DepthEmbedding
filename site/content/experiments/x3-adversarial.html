Where along depth does an adversarial image leave its class?

Tested: Final CIFAR-10 ResNet-20 checkpoints. Seed 0 was attacked; seed 1 was used for transfer. The {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.cohort.n}} test images are outside the {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.probe.n}}-image probe and both seeds classify them correctly when clean. The embedding uses the archived seed 0 inputs. A separate kernel check uses a matched regenerated cohort.

For the untargeted attack at 8/255, the median persistent class switch was at {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.switch.u8.median}}, but switches were spread across depth. Median displacement matched sign noise to two decimal places at b6 ({{fact:x.x3-adversarial.disp.u8.b6}} vs {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.disp.n8.b6}} same-class units) and pulled away at b7 ({{fact:x.x3-adversarial.disp.u8.b7}} vs {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.disp.n8.b7}}).

Caveats

  • audit Joint coordinates mix sites. Replacing only the logits row gives {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.logits_only.stem}} of the full attack's median joint displacement at stem and {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.logits_only.b9}} at b9 (Shannon, first {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.metric.k}} modes, rather than all fitted modes as in the appendix). Earlier site-local readouts change by {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.logits_only.earlier_site_local}}.
  • audit Equal $L^\infty$ budgets do not match $L^2$ size. The median centred-activation cosine already differs at b6 ({{fact:x.x3-adversarial.activation.u8.b6.cosine}} attack vs {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.activation.n8.b6.cosine}} noise). No $L^2$-matched noise control was run.
  • Some walk modes concentrate on very few images. The archived Shannon maximum is {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.localisation.ea100.site_max}} on two probe images ({{fact:x.x3-adversarial.localisation.ea100.site_at}}, walk mode {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.localisation.ea100.site_mode}}). The matched regenerated cohort gives {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.rerun.concentration.ea100.site}} with Shannon and {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.rerun.concentration.pr100.site}} with participation ratio. The late displacement gap and typical switch sites survive; exact distances and class masses change.
  • The archived attacks did not regenerate bit-for-bit. At 8/255, seed 0's final prediction matched the archive on {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.rerun.agreement.u8.predictions}} of untargeted attacks and {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.rerun.agreement.n8.predictions}} of noise inputs. Only {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.rerun.agreement.u8.pixels}} of rounded attack pixel-channel values matched. All kernels in the new comparison use the same regenerated inputs.
  • audit A switch is a rule on class mass, not a boundary crossing. The stricter rule moves the median to {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.switch.u8.top_median}}. Under the true-class rule, {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.switch.u8.stem}} already meet it at the stem, where masses are close to uniform; {{fact:x.x3-adversarial.switch.natural.never}} of natural errors never meet it persistently.
  • One architecture, two training seeds, one probe. No bandwidth sweep, fresh probe or additional architecture in the original X3 run.
