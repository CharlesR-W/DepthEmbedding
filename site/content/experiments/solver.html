How far can the joint operator's solver scale?

Engineering note · Benchmarked on the 12-site, 2,000-image CIFAR-10 probe (ResNet-20 seed 0, k-NN 15), on a 4-vCPU VPS under concurrent load. The prototypes were not applied to the pipeline.

The k-NN walk can be stored sparsely without changing the operator. Its compact form reduced walk storage and sped up the prototype solver at 2,000 images. Larger-probe times and RAM are projections, not completed runs.

Caveats

  • Walk storage is not peak process memory. The prototype still reads precomputed Grams and finds neighbours; these timings exclude the cost of computing the Grams.
  • Subtracting a slice mean does not remove the rank-one term unless $d$ is constant. Dropping the floor instead changed the first 16 eigenvalues by up to {{fact:x.solver.floor_drift}}% in the study.
  • The exact compact representation and sparse Sinkhorn do not carry over to the smooth kernel. Partial spectra, bounded Krylov memory and faster dense linear algebra are separate options. Sparsifying a smooth kernel would change the operator.
