Which kernel gives modes that reproduce and a smooth row for a new image?

Tested: Final CIFAR-10 ResNet-20, seed 0. P2 is the viewer's {{fact:x.inv-kernel.probe.p2}}-image test probe; P8 adds more test images to P2. We vary the within-site kernel and balance the depth coupling separately for each operator.

Participation ratio was the best candidate in these runs: it avoided the two-image modes seen with Shannon perplexity and retained a smooth out-of-sample rule away from ties. It also had stronger half-split scores at the settings shown, but its final walk was wider. The experiment did not isolate the effect of the rule from the effect of width.

Caveats

  • In the larger Shannon run, {{fact:x.inv-kernel.top2.p8.ea400_count}} of the first 64 joint modes exceeded 10% two-image share. The two images in a localised mode are not necessarily duplicates; the experiment did not establish the mechanism.
  • Audit: The PR bound applies before symmetrisation and Sinkhorn. The zero diagonal makes masked probe replay exact up to numerical error, but a near-copy of a probe image can receive a large weight on that image.
  • One network, one checkpoint and one probe draw at each size. No matched-width sweep, repeated splits or uncertainty intervals. Increasing N also changed the adaptive width target.
