Caveats

  • In the larger Shannon run, {{fact:x.inv-kernel.top2.p8.ea400_count}} of the first 64 joint modes exceeded 10% two-image share. The two images in a localised mode are not necessarily duplicates; the experiment did not establish the mechanism.
  • Held-out joint refit (Shannon 300 vs PR 300): K = 16 scores were {{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k16.ea300.min}}–{{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k16.ea300.max}} vs {{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k16.pr300.min}}–{{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k16.pr300.max}}; Shannon led at all {{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k16.shannon_wins}} sites. At K = 64: {{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k64.ea300.min}}–{{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k64.ea300.max}} vs {{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k64.pr300.min}}–{{fact:x.inv-kernel.refit.k64.pr300.max}}. The run refits on P2+H with doubled targets and aligns sample-size-rescaled coordinates by one orthogonal map fitted on P2 across sites. The score is one minus squared error relative to refit energy; it includes refit drift, not just extension error.
  • In the local split, PR beat Shannon at {{fact:x.inv-kernel.split.pr_over_shannon}} of {{fact:x.inv-kernel.split.sites}} sites (not b9). The unplotted global-width comparison beat PR at {{fact:x.inv-kernel.split.global_over_pr}} sites.

Method concerns from the audit

  • audit Limit — one shared-centre, fixed-target split. A and B share the full-P2 centring mean; half-size walks keep the absolute target while the plotted widths come from full P2. Repeat in both directions with independent fit centres, training-centred cross rows and measured half-walk widths.
  • audit Limit — localisation depends on the basis. In a nearly degenerate eigenspace, individual modes can rotate. Check localisation of the whole subspace using image leverage.
  • audit Possible flaw — probe and query rows differ. Masked replay checks the fitted convention only: a new near-copy is unmasked, and the frozen reverse affinity with its old normaliser can exceed one. Test modified affinities separately from masked replay.
